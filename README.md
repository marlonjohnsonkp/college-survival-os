### Project Description

Survival OS is a playful student dashboard for surviving college chaos. It brings attendance, assignments, money, procrastination, relationships, roommate drama, and questionable decisions into one entertaining place.

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)

College life has too many small disasters and not enough dramatic dashboards to announce them. Students are left to track deadlines, empty wallets, and suspicious "K" messages with zero unnecessary technology.

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)

Survival OS turns student chaos into a fun control room. It roasts your money habits, watches your procrastination, translates risky messages, and gives AI-powered survival advice — all with a Manglish student vibe.
